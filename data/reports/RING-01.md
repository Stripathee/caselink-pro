## Summary

A police impersonation scam ring operating from 2026-01-06 to 2026-08-01 targeted 149 victims across Nepal, impersonating 'Inspector Shrestha' and other officers from the Central Investigation Bureau to extort NPR 47,649,000 through 'digital arrest' threats. Victims were kept on hours-long video calls and pressured to pay security deposits into two Morivan Finance bank accounts and three IME Pay wallets. [F1] [F2] [F3] [F4] [F6]
